About me

6+ years in design

Based in Kraków, working globally

End-to-end ownership

I am a Product Designer with over 6 years of experience in UX/UI, motion, visual and graphic design, as well as product strategy. I design mobile and web applications, AI tools, chatbots and smartwatch experiences for transportation, logistics, eCommerce and retail.

I lead the entire design process, from research to implementation, ensuring user-centric solutions.

As a mentor, I enjoy sharing knowledge and guiding others in their design journey. Inspired by nature, music and true crime stories.

Skills

User experience

Designing products that are intuitive, user-friendly and deliver value by focusing on the overall user journey and interaction.

UI design & design systems

Interfaces with attention to detail and scalable design systems that keep products consistent across platforms and devices.

Visual design

Aesthetically pleasing digital products through thoughtful colour, typography, layout and visual branding.

Product strategy

Defining the vision, roadmap and business goals of the product so design aligns with strategy and market needs.

Motion design

Animations and micro-interactions that make interfaces feel alive and improve engagement.

Research & usability testing

User research and usability tests that validate decisions and improve product effectiveness.
